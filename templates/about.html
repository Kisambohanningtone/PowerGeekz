Who We Are

Kenya's Leading
green Energy Power Solutions Provider

Powergeekz Engineering Ltd is a leading provider of power backup and power protection systems across the region. Based in Nairobi since 2013, we have the expertise to take a client's basic requirements and convert them into systems that meet or exceed expectations.

We are authorized dealers for most of the equipment we supply, enabling us to guarantee quality, genuine products and full manufacturer warranty support on everything we install.

Our passion for excellence means we assure all clients of efficient, professional service from initial consultation through to after-sales support — whether you are a domestic home or a 300-bed hospital.
